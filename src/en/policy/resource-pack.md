## Preface and Important Notes
This document is an appendix to the "Souton Server Regulations." However, due to the incompleteness of the relevant clauses, this document is currently treated as an independent regulation. These regulations apply to the "Souton Server" (hereinafter referred to as "Souton") and the "Souton Server Resource Pack" (hereinafter referred to as "Souton Resource Pack"). As the content of the server resource pack is becoming increasingly rich, and some paid resource pack content has been authorized for integration, this regulation is hereby established to prevent unauthorized use of this resource pack.

This document was written by JW and edited by DeepSeek and ChatGPT. For inquiries, please contact the "Souton Management Group" (i.e., individuals with "Owner"/"Admin" status on the "Discord" server or administrator privileges in the "QQ" communication group).

## Chapter 1 Resource Packs

- The Shuton Resource Pack is for use only on the Shuton server. It may not be used outside of Shuton (including but not limited to private save files or other servers) without the prior written consent of the Shuton administrator.

- Players may not disclose, sell, attempt to sell, offer to sell, or invite others to purchase any modpacks issued by or on behalf of Shuton outside of Shuton.

- Players are limited to playing the Shuton Resource Pack and may not make any changes to the contents of the resource pack in any form (including but not limited to changing blocks, train liveries, modifying sound effects, etc.).

- Any person who violates the above provisions will be prosecuted by the Shuton management team in accordance with Chapter 528 of the Laws of Hong Kong, the Copyright Ordinance. Any non-Hong Kong users will be pursued in accordance with local copyright laws.

Copyright Regulations: https://www.elegislation.gov.hk/hk/cap528

## Notes
Shuton shall not be liable for any user violations of the above regulations (including but not limited to the paid add-on content within the Shuton resource pack), and all consequences shall be borne by the user. Shuton reserves the right to report any user violations to other servers and block the user's account.

For matters not covered in these regulations, the "Shuton Management Team" (i.e., those holding the "Server Owner"/"Admin" status on the "Discord" server or administrator privileges in the "QQ" communication group) has the final right to interpret, decide on, and enforce these regulations.
